# 🛡️ SafeGuard Identity

## Core Values
The SafeGuard design system is built on three pillars that reflect the mission of Matafuegos Necochea:

1.  **Safety & Urgency**: Utilizing high-visibility colors (Safety Red) to denote critical information and emergency actions.
2.  **Trust & Reliability**: A solid, structured layout that conveys professionalism and legal compliance.
3.  **Precision & Clarity**: Architectural minimalism that ensures users can find critical safety certifications and expiration dates instantly.

## Design Philosophy

### Architectural Minimalism
Layouts are structured and predictable. We avoid unnecessary decorative elements that could distract from critical data. Every element serves a functional purpose.

### Journalistic Gravitas
Using **Outfit** for headings provides a modern yet authoritative feel. High contrast between text and background ensures accessibility in all lighting conditions—critical for field technicians working in diverse environments.

### Premium Solid Finish
We favor solid blocks of color and sharp borders over gradients or soft shadows. This conveys a sense of stability and permanence, matching the physical nature of safety equipment.
